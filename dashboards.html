CFPB Consumer Complaint Analysis

Project Overview

This dashboard provides a comprehensive visual analysis of over 8.3 million consumer complaints filed with the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) between January 2025 and July 2026. By structuring and visualizing this massive dataset, the report identifies systemic issues, geographic pain points, and company-specific trends within the financial sector.

Key Insights Discovered

  • Credit Bureaus Dominate the Data: The vast majority of consumer grievances are directed at the "Big Three" credit reporting agencies. TransUnion, Equifax, and Experian account for over 7.1 million of the total complaints analyzed.
  • Root Cause Identification: Utilizing decomposition tree analysis, the data reveals that "Credit reporting" is the primary product of concern. Drilling down further, "Incorrect information" acts as the leading sub-issue, driving over 4.3 million specific complaints.
  • Volume Trends & Geography: The 3-month rolling average highlights a steady, continuous upward trend in complaint volume from early 2025 through mid-2026. Additionally, geospatial tracking maps out regional densities of delayed company responses (taking over 3 days).
